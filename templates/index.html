Install Aayatun on iPhone

Safari cannot install apps by itself. Add Aayatun to your Home Screen so it opens full screen in one tap:

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari's toolbar.
  2. Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add, then open Aayatun from your Home Screen.

If the Share menu has no Add to Home Screen option, open this page in Safari first.
